WHAT JARVIS DOES

One robot, four ways to talk to it.

Jarvis runs core movement locally, and reaches out to the internet for anything that needs a brain.

01

Voice activation

Say "Hey Siri, start the Jarvis" from up to 10 meters away — Jarvis wakes up and introduces itself, in English, Hindi, and Nepali.

02

Telegram control

Send movement commands or ask Jarvis a question from anywhere with internet — not just on local Wi-Fi.

03

Mobile app control

A Blynk app dashboard with forward, back, left, right and stop — direct control over the local network.

04

Expressive display

An OLED face that switches between happy, funny, confused, angry, thinking and neutral expressions as Jarvis talks.

05

Obstacle sensing

An ultrasonic sensor stops the robot automatically before it runs into something, regardless of what command is running.
